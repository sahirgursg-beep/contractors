NAICS 237990: Other Heavy and Civil Engineering Construction

SBA small business size standard: $45 million in average annual receipts. Effective March 17, 2023.

SBA size standard

Use the average of the business’s receipts over its last five completed fiscal years, including affiliates. This is a quick screen, not an official size determination.

What the size standard means

A business whose primary industry is NAICS 237990 counts as small for federal contracting and most SBA programs if its average annual receipts are at or below $45 million. Receipts are averaged over the business’s latest five completed fiscal years, and the receipts of affiliated companies are added in.

Federal contracting officers assign one NAICS code to each solicitation. When you bid on a small business set-aside, you must be small under the code on that solicitation, which may differ from your primary code. You represent your size for each NAICS code in your SAM.gov registration.

Proposed change, not yet in effect. On August 20, 2026 SBA proposed new size standards that would move construction from receipts-based to employee-based limits. Until a final rule takes effect, the current standards on this page apply.

Read the proposed rule in the Federal Register.
