Michigan contractor license requirements

Yes, for residential work. Michigan requires a Residential Builder or Maintenance & Alteration Contractor license from LARA for residential jobs of $600 or more. There is no state license for commercial general contracting.

Two residential licenses

  • Residential Builder: new homes and any residential construction, remodeling or repair.
  • Maintenance & Alteration (M&A) Contractor: limited to the specific trades listed on the license, such as roofing, siding, carpentry or basement waterproofing.

How to get licensed

  1. Complete 60 hours of approved prelicensure education, with at least 6 hours in each required subject, such as business management and estimating, and design and building science.
  2. Apply to LARA online or on paper.
  3. After LARA approves the application, pass the state exam, which is given by PSI.

Electrical, plumbing and mechanical work need separate trade licenses. Commercial general contracting has no state license, but local permits and codes still apply.
